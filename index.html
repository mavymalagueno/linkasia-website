About us

A Licensed Agency Built on Compliance and Results

Established on June 6, 2006, Link Asia Manpower Solutions Corp. is a land-based recruitment agency licensed by the Philippine Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) under License No. DMW-067-LB-03312023-R. From our office in Madrigal Business Park, Alabang, we serve foreign employers across the Middle East, Asia, Europe, Canada and the Caribbean.

With two decades in overseas recruitment, our team has deployed more than 8,000 Filipino workers since 2017 under current management — handling every stage of the process, from sourcing and screening to documentation and deployment, in full compliance with Philippine migrant worker regulations.

  • DMW-licensed, land-based recruitment
  • Full-service: sourcing to deployment
  • Compliance-first documentation
  • Dedicated employer support

Recruitment process

A Clear Path from Job Order to Deployment

Our structured process ensures every worker is properly screened, documented and deployed in full compliance with Philippine and destination-country regulations.

1

Job order

You send us your manpower requirements and job order details.

2

Sourcing

We source candidates from our nationwide applicant network.

3

Screening

Candidates are interviewed, trade-tested and shortlisted to your specifications.

4

Documentation

We handle DMW-compliant contracts, visas and exit clearances.

5

Deployment

Workers are briefed, mobilised and deployed to your worksite.
